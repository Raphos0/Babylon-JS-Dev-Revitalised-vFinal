About Me

I'm David Kazanowski of Dark Forge Games, a University student studying at the University of the West of Scotland under Games Development in 3rd Year. I specialise in game programming, in languages such as C#, C++, Javascript, HTML and more. However, I also have experience with other skills such as 3D Modelling in Blender.



Here are some of my previous projects:


Gears of Blood

A game where you awake as a puppet, designed to hunt beasts in place of human hunters by the Father of all Puppets. Gears of Blood is a level-based, 3rd person hack and slash game where you venture through a series of areas in the city of Nfynham, fighting your path through killing hordes of enemies, until you face off the boss that awaits at the end of the area.

Sanctum Arcana

A 3D first person shooter, wave defence game, where you and friends play as wizards tasked with defending the last and greatest of the Aether Trees, the Starbloom. Every wizard has now come to defend the Starbloom and ensure it does not fall to the hand of enemy forces.

Tenement 49

A tense puzzle game where you, the detective, must discover the mystery behind your brother, who went missing as he was working on his latest invention. Set in an Alternate Victorian Era London, you arrive at the entrance of the tenement, tasked with exploring every nook and cranny to solve a variety of puzzles.

Untitled Gladiator Game

An action RPG where you play as a gladiator, favoured by the gods themselves, fighting their way through gauntlets of enemies to become the grand champion and regain their freedom. In the arena, you will face 4 waves of enemies, scavenging their weapons to use against them until you fight the champion of the gauntlet, increasing your rank upon victory.
